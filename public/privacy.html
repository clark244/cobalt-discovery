Privacy Policy

Cobalt Collective — Impact Discovery tool · Effective 25 August 2026 · Version 1.0

This policy explains what the Cobalt Collective Impact Discovery tool ("the tool") collects, how we use it, and your choices. Cobalt Collective is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit incorporated in Delaware, United States, and is the controller of the personal data described here. The tool is a free, advisory resource — it is not a funding application and using it is not a request for or promise of funding.

1. Information we collect

Identity you provideYour name (or initials) and email address, which you enter to begin. We email a one-time code to that address to confirm it is yours.
Your conversationThe full text of your discovery conversation with the tool, including everything you type about your organization, product, and plans, and the tool's responses and generated assessment.
Referral / traffic sourceHow you arrived at the tool — the referring web address and any campaign ("UTM") tags in the link you followed.
Optional follow-up emailIf you choose to leave an email at the end for a follow-up or updates, we store that separately.
Technical dataYour IP address and basic request information, used for security and to enforce usage limits.

Please do not enter information you are not authorized to share, and avoid submitting sensitive personal data (for example, health, financial, or identifying details about specific individuals). The tool is designed for describing your organization and product, not for personal records.

2. How we use it

  • To run the discovery conversation and generate your impact model, measurement plan, and summary.
  • To verify your email, prevent abuse, and enforce daily usage limits.
  • To understand how the tool is used and to improve it and Cobalt's services.
  • To follow up with you about Cobalt's work if you request it, or if you choose to start a conversation with us.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to make an automated funding or eligibility decision about you. A person at Cobalt reviews sessions.

3. AI processing

The conversation is processed by a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to generate the tool's responses and your assessment. Your inputs are sent to that provider to deliver the service. We do not use your conversation to train foundation models.

4. Where your information is stored and who sees it

Session records — including your name, email, transcript, and generated assessment — are stored in Cobalt's Google Workspace (Google Sheets) and are accessible to authorized Cobalt staff. Cobalt staff may receive a notification when a session is completed. We use reputable service providers (including our website host and email provider) to operate the tool.

5. International data transfers

Cobalt Collective operates from the United States. Your information is transferred to and processed in the United States, which may not offer the same level of data protection as your home country. Our principal sub-processors are:

  • Anthropic (United States) — AI processing of your conversation to generate the tool's responses and your assessment.
  • Google (United States) — storage of session records in Google Workspace.
  • Our website host and email provider, which help us deliver the site and send verification and follow-up emails.

For personal data transferred from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we rely on our providers' certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (together with the UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as applicable), which the European Commission has recognized as providing an adequate level of protection for such transfers. Where a provider's Data Privacy Framework certification does not apply, lapses, or is invalidated, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses as the safeguard for the transfer. You can request more information about these safeguards using the contact details below.

6. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

Processing activityLegal basis
Running the discovery conversation and generating your assessmentPerformance of the service you request; and our legitimate interest in providing the tool's core functionality.
Verifying your email and preventing abuseOur legitimate interest in security and platform integrity.
Analytics and improving the tool and our servicesOur legitimate interest in improving the service — you may object to this processing at any time (see "Your choices and rights").
Follow-up communications about Cobalt's workYour consent, given when you opt in by leaving a follow-up email. You may withdraw consent at any time.

7. Retention

We retain session records for 24 months following your most recent session with the tool. When that retention period ends, records are permanently deleted on a scheduled basis — no later than 30 days after expiry. If you leave a follow-up email to hear from us, we keep it until you ask us to stop or withdraw your consent. You may ask us to delete your information sooner at any time (see below); we will action deletion requests within a reasonable period and instruct our sub-processors to do the same, except where we are required by law to retain certain records.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can request a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing clark@cobaltcollective.org.
  • You can object to processing we carry out on the basis of legitimate interest (including analytics and service improvement), and you can withdraw consent to follow-up communications at any time.
  • You can decline to leave a follow-up email; the follow-up is optional.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA — including rights to access, portability, restriction, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Contact us to exercise them.

9. Children

The tool is intended for adults (18+) using it in a professional or organizational capacity. It is not directed to children and should not be used to submit information about children.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes

We will update this policy as the tool evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
